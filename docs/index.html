CorrecTMe

Types the right thing when your keyboard is in the wrong language.

You meant to type English, but the keyboard was still set to Hebrew. CorrecTMe notices and fixes it as you go, so you do not retype the sentence.

you typed  פךקשדק רקהןק'
you get     please review

How it works

  • Corrects as you type, or only when you ask — your choice.
  • Select any text anywhere and press one shortcut to flip it between layouts.
  • Hebrew, Russian and English.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes any correction.

Privacy

CorrecTMe runs entirely on your own machine and makes no network requests at all — no telemetry, no analytics, no update pinging. Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.
